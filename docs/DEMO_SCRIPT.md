# Demo script

Under two minutes. Point-of-view: one person in a stockroom, wearing Even G2 glasses, with an R1 ring on the counting hand. The other hand can stay on a case of bottles. Phone stays in a pocket. A second camera can show the owner’s Telegram chat at the end. No screen recording of a laptop is required for the story, though the simulator recording is the fallback if the headset is not in the room.

Target length: 1 minute 45 seconds.

## 0:00 – 0:12 · The clipboard

Wide shot of a short aisle. Four products with shelf labels: Coke 50cl, water, milk, oats. The counter is holding a clipboard and has to put a case down to write.

Voice, quiet, to camera: “We still count like this. Both hands leave the shelf, and the owner hears about the gap tomorrow.”

## 0:12 – 0:28 · The card is already there

Cut to the wearer’s view. The glasses show Aisle B, shelf B3, Coke 50cl, expected 24, count 24, and the line `Scroll +/-1 · Tap -> Confirm · Dbl tap -> Skip`.

Voice: “Recount reads this aisle from Notion. The ring is on my hand. I don’t put the box down.”

## 0:28 – 0:48 · Ring, then confirm

Scroll the ring down three times. The count goes 23, 22, 21. Tap. The confirm card reads `21 (-3 SHORT)`. Tap again. The card says saved, with a three-second undo. Let it pass.

Voice: “Twenty-one. I’m short three. Tap to look, tap to save. If I mis-tap, I undo before it sticks.”

## 0:48 – 1:08 · Say the next one

Next card: Water 75cl, expected 40. Hold the ring and say “thirty-eight, one damaged.” The confirm card shows 38, −2 short, 1 damaged. Tap to save.

Voice: “Or I just say it. Two boxes of six would land as twelve. It does not write until I tap.”

## 1:08 – 1:22 · Match, skip, send

Milk is correct. Tap, tap, let the undo pass. Oats: double-tap to skip. The summary reads 3 counted, variance −5. Tap once.

Voice: “Milk matches. I’ll come back to the oats. One tap sends the aisle.”

## 1:22 – 1:45 · The owner’s phone

Cut to the owner’s Telegram. The message is from the shop bot. Short items are first: Coke 21/24 (−3), damaged if any, then water, then the milk that matched. The skipped oats are counted as skipped, not as zero.

Voice: “She sees the shortage before she sees the shelf. The count is already on the Notion row.”

End on the glasses card: `REPORT SENT`.

## If this is shot in the simulator

Use `npm run dev`, then `npm run sim`, and film the simulator window plus the shop page. Say on camera that the ring gestures are the simulator’s scroll and tap, and that the spoken line is injected because the simulator microphone is not reliable. Do not claim the simulator is the headset.
